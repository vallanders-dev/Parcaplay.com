title: "v0.1.0 — the beta begins"
date: "2026-09-25"
summary: "First beta release: voice questions with F8, notes with F6, answers that start speaking right away, and interrupt anytime."
version: "v0.1.0"
---

Parça opens its doors. In this first version you can press F8 and ask out loud — it looks at the game window, finds the answer and starts speaking as soon as the first sentence is ready, without making you wait for the whole thing.

With F6 you save voice notes ("my base is behind the waterfall"): it reads the note back and only saves it after your clear "yes".

And if it's talking and you think of something else, just press F8 again — it stops right away.
