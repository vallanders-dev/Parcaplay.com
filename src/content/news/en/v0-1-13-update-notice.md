**Version 0.1.13.** Parça now tells you when a new version is out: a notice appears in the corner of the screen with a **Restart** button, and seconds later you're up to date. Updates come straight from Parça's server and every file is checked before it's installed.

To get this version, one last time: right-click the Parça icon by the clock → **Quit**, then open Parça again. From here on, it takes care of the rest by itself.
