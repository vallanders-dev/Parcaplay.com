Parça now looks like a real program: a main window, a Windows tray icon with options to open, pause, change settings and quit.

The glowing orb appears in the corner of the screen while you play, showing whether it's ready, listening, thinking or speaking — and how many questions you have left today.

And when a game runs as administrator, one click relaunches Parça the right way.
